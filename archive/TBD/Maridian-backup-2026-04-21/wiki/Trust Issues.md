## Trust Issues

**Core Belief:**
I've learned that trust is a luxury only afforded to those who have been hurt the least, and even then, it's often conditional.

**How This Evolved:**
- [2022]: I began to realize that my friends' behavior towards me was not about me, but about their own emotional unavailability.
- [2024]: I started to understand that my inability to trust others stems from the fact that I've been hurt by those who were supposed to be supportive and caring.

**In His Own Words:**
