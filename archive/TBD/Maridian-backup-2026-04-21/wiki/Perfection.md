## Perfection

**Core Belief:**
I believe perfection is unattainable and that the pursuit of it can be paralyzing.

**How This Evolved:**
- [2026-02-19]: The desire for perfection stems from a lack of clarity on one's goals, leading to an inability to commit to a single path.
- [2026-02-19]: Recognizing the fear of committing to one path without a clear vision.

**In His Own Words:**
> "I want to be able to see it and envision me being successful, something I can’t do right now because I don’t know what it looks like."
