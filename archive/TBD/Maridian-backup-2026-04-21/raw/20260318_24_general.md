Life is very poetic. In the same way good always triumph evil. God beats satan. Light beats dark. By that I mean it is literally impossible to have darkness in light. But the same goes for the other way around. So you must always be aware of what side you are on. If a room is dark and you turn on the light switch it cannot be dark unless that switch is turned off, it can be turned off by you or by someone else. The differentiator is your response. Do you sit in the temporary comfort of the dark, like how when you sleep and have dreams you like for it to be dark. But when you want to live and be involved in the world, you must step into the light. The interwoveness of the world is incredible, everything is connected and so well thought out.
