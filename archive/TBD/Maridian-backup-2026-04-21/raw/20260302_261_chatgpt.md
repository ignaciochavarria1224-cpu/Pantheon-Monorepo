[AI Business Strategies]

You are a ruthless but realistic AI business strategist.

I am 18 years old, a finance student at FSU in Tallahassee. I am not a programmer, but I understand AI tools (ChatGPT, basic prompting, etc.).

My goal is to make my first $1,000 online within 30 days.

Constraints:
	•	No coding-heavy businesses
	•	No oversaturated generic ideas (no dropshipping, no basic SMMA, no print-on-demand)
	•	Low upfront cost
	•	Must be realistic for a college student
	•	Must leverage AI as a force multiplier

Context:
	•	I’m willing to call businesses, DM people, or sell a service.
	•	I care more about speed and execution than perfection.
	•	I want something scalable beyond $1,000 if it works.

Your task:
	1.	Identify 3 AI-powered opportunities that are currently underexploited in local markets or niche online markets.
	2.	For each, explain:
	•	Why it’s not saturated yet
	•	Why I personally have an advantage
	•	Exact first 7-day execution plan
	•	How I would realistically close my first client
	3.	Choose ONE as the highest probability path and explain why.
	4.	Give me the first uncomfortable action I must take within 24 hours.

Be direct. No fluff. No motivational speeches. Only strategy.
