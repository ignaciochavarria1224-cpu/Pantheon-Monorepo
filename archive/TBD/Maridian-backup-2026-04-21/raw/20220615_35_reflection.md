God I always ask myself why doesn’t he answer. But everything that ever made me feel sad, happy, angry it’s for a reason. For me I don’t think I have ever realized how god truly does his work. All of those good things happening to you is God. Like the relationship with my friends. When I wasn’t friends with them I was praying all the time for god to give me my friends back I just want to go hangout with them leave my house and stop being so alone. During that time I did stuff I shouldn’t have done. But I was also doing good things. Spent more time with my family, I would workout everyday but I wasn’t thinking how good it was for me. All I could think about is how I wanted to be with my friends instead of appreciating the present. Now that I am back with my friends and hear how mike Tyson said “God gives you everything you want to see if you can handle it.” I am not handling what god is giving me right now. I have my family, now my cousins and grandma are here. I have my friends again. I am healthy and so is everyone else I care for. I have everything I would want yet I am still thinking about the future and am still messing up in school just cause I want to go out. Clearly God is showing me how I can’t handle what I prayed for. Moral of the story God will always guide you in the right path but you will not be handheld the whole way he will just be there with you and show you what way to go. You need to decide what you will do.
