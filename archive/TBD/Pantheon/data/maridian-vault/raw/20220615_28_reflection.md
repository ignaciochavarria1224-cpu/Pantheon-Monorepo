Talking to god today he showed me things about the world that I haven’t seen. He told me to go to the pool not to swim but to just breathe. I obliged I got in the pool did stretching and also breathing and staring at the sky. What I saw was one of one I saw god showing me how the world works. So to begin with god showed me the dark clouds. He told me that these are the bad moments in your life. Something like when your parents are fighting a lot. But god has already set the mark on your life where you are going to succeed and well the journey is your choice. It could be good or it could be bad. That depends on you and only you. But remember that these success marks in your life that god placed will be there so you just have to be strong and wait until you get there. Although it might seem as though he is saying to just go through loads of pain or maybe even if you have a good life but you still want to listen to him speak. God simply says that if there is pain you will have to suffer and continue on to the next challenge but if there is no pain then thank him and keep going don’t try and create conflict to fill that gap and give yourself an excuse to use when you fail. Because yes you will fail over and over again but that is life you have to get up and keep going once more. And it starts on the planet we live on you hear the wind gushing but feels like a light cool breeze you see an owl it’s making noises you see the process in which life itself is the birds fighting the little kids how free they are have little knowledge of life yet, they have no worries. These little kids today there was 3, 2 of which were boys and of course one girl and they kept on saying bye to each other and would not stop they were down the street and still saying bye to each other. And then the soothing noise of the water as the swimmer do their sets is incredible it’s all calms your head and this is another world that is not discovered by many and can calm any mind this is one of god success points for me. This is all what I feel that god showed me just by going to the pool today.
