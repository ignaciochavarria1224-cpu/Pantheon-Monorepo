## Free Will Debate

**Core Belief:**
I believe we have free will, but it's influenced by outside conditions.

**How This Evolved:**
- 2025-08-26: I'm starting to consider the idea that free will might not be as absolute as I thought, and that external factors can impact our decisions.
- 2025-08-26 (later): I've tied this concept to my Christianity, suggesting that even if we don't have true free will, we still have a purpose that guides us.

**In His Own Words:**
> "I guess you would still have a purpose, because your purpose would be fulfilling that path that you're meant to go down."
