I have an app idea and have already coded it I just want to know if there is something that could be added or a feature that will elevate the app
This is the summary of the app: 

NeuralPulse is an AI-powered news app that automatically discovers and delivers the latest advances in artificial intelligence every day.
What it does: Once per day, it searches the web using the Anthropic API, finds the most relevant and interesting AI news and use cases, and surfaces them as clean, readable summaries on a personalized home feed — each with a link to the full original article.
Who uses it: You log in with an account, land on your feed, and can browse articles filtered by category (Healthcare, AI Agents, Model Releases, etc.). Every article is summarized so you get the key insight in 30 seconds, with the option to go deeper.
The chatbot: A built-in AI assistant that’s fully aware of every article in that day’s feed. You can ask it to explain a concept from an article, compare two stories, discuss implications, or go on a deep dive — it’s essentially a knowledgeable guide to the day’s news.
The tech: React frontend hosted on Vercel, Supabase for auth and database, a daily GitHub Actions job that calls the Anthropic API with web search to populate fresh articles each morning, and Claude powering the chatbot.
The pitch in one sentence: It’s a self-updating AI research digest with a built-in expert you can have a conversation with — for about $2–5/month to run.​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​
