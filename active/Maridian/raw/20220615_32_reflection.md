Each day is a chapter in your book just as each second is a word. You can breeze by the book or you can relax yourself and pay attention to the book or you can sweep by and when you get to the end you are lost and confused. So sit down and read every page of that book don’t let things distract you and just try and make each day count. Make every word in that book of life mean something. Switch up your life. Stop doing the things you know you shouldn’t do. You are basing your life on other peoples. You are special all you have to do is whatever God tells you. You know what God is saying is right and it is what you should be doing. Although while I’m getting this thought I’m am committing the very sins i wish not to commit. I can no longer go with doing things that I don’t want to do. Since I know that I will not quit now but later. If I set a due date then I am putting all my trust in Jesus and God that there will be a sign for me to come back and read this. I will remember but I still would love to see a sign I will go on the terrace and look outside. This will be the place where Jesus I will stop committing all these sins and commit to you. On January 1, 2023 I will be there. Hope to see you there too.
