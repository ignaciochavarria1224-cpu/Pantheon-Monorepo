## Social Contract

**Core Belief:**
I believe that a social contract can be both present and limiting, creating distance between individuals and hindering vulnerability.

**How This Evolved:**
- [2024]: The concept of social contract emerged as a recurring theme in my journal entries, with me questioning its existence and impact on relationships.
- [2024-12]: I began to recognize the ways in which social contract can create distance and silence, making it challenging for others to be vulnerable with me.

**In His Own Words:**
"I feel like it 100% creates a distance between me and people, and it's like a distance that we both know that we have, like people don't talk to me about their relationships or who they made out with and stuff like that."
