## Balance

**Core Belief:**
I believe balance is the key to making meaningful decisions and finding stability in life.

**How This Evolved:**
- [2024]: I started realizing that extremes are often unstable and emotional, and that balance is necessary for clarity and good decision-making.
- [2024-10]: I learned about the Law of the Middle, which suggests that everything meaningful sits in the middle, and that balance is what allows us to control our impulses and make better choices.

**In His Own Words:**
> "I believe just everything's about balance."
