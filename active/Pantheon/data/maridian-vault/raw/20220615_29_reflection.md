Our parents 

They take risks big and small Not for them but for us. Take food of their plate to feed our mouths. Go hungry suffer just so you can hate. Be great full for all you are given For soon you will realize how hard life truly is once you really start living. You will soon realize how much you should have listened But if you remember they said you will truly glisten. All you have to do is be yourself and you will shine Follow your passion and stay in line. Just follow the rules and always listen Because they have been through the tough times so you don’t have to. You might stray away for some time but you will always return to them For they know what’s best. No one loves you more, you can truly feel it in ur chest. They have created you and it’s not cause of that; that they love you But cause of all the good times and joy they enjoy with you. You have given them laughter and have helped clear their head Cause even when you do something wrong there is always a lesson at the end of the story. You might get these feeling that are not always good Remember to talk to them their always there for you. They love you more than the money they might desire You truly are their own creation a part of them. Although this is all true they want to see you shine They also hope you live your own life and your own dreams. Follow those dreams and don’t give up because even when times are tough as they taught you you will get through it and it all gets better. Like when it’s cold all you do is put on a sweater and wait for it to get hotter. That’s what you must do during the hard times put on your sweater suffer a little but you know that soon it will get better. And the sweater in your life consists of the people that love you and if you think no one does you are greatly mistaken your parents are people that will never leave you vacant. They will love you even when they are gone. But just remember they will always look down at you from heaven remembering and admiring the life you are living. Don’t think cause you made a mistake your life is over it all passes by and they won’t ignore you. Just use that mistake to see where people stand if they don’t back you up you know who they are. Your parents will help you so don’t hold back don’t lie to them. Whatever you think you did even the worst you can imagine they will help you through without judging. You must remember you will always be loved by your parents and by god. He is always there and so are they. Even if you don’t pray don’t worry he forgives all. All you must do is start now don’t care about what others think and trust him cause he has your plan all written out and even when you think why me. think about it and you will see. There is always a lesson in everything you do. God sees everything and knows it all so something you don’t know and he does he helped you fix it. Put all your trust in god and you will succeed. And god helped shape your parents too, so those are like his disciples they tell you what you need to hear. Final lesson trust god and trust your parents because they will never fail you even when u don’t think it will work everything happens for a reason so you being born to those parents you got happened for a reason. You will never disappoint nor will they ever be disappointed in you. Live your life how you want to and not how other tell you that you should.
