[1-Month Life Optimization]

Experiment 
1 month data optimization 

Details: 
I want to live my life for a month. For this month I will track certain parts of my life. This data will be recorded, I will then input this data into an AI every week on Sunday. I will also record at the end of every day a short summary of my day and how I felt throughout the day. 

I will be tracking: 
1.) Energy 
	◦	Energy level 1/10
	◦	Hours of sleep 
	◦	Screen time
2.) Body 
	◦	Workout (Yes/No, what I hit) 
	◦	Steps 
	◦	Cardio (Yes/No, what I hit) 
3.) Mind
	◦	Mental clarity 1/10
	◦	Meditation yes/no how long 
	◦	Pray yes/no 
4.) Money 
	◦	Net worth 
	◦	Cashflow 
	◦	What new financial decision was made 
5.) Direction 
	◦	Alignment score 1/10
	◦	Good deed
	◦	Am I moving forward or backwards?
