## Freedom

**Core Belief:**
I've come to realize that true freedom lies in breaking free from external expectations and internal constraints, allowing me to forge my own path and define my purpose in life.

**How This Evolved:**
- [2026]: I initially struggled with the concept of freedom, feeling held back by societal expectations and personal responsibilities. However, as I reflected on my relationships and goals, I began to understand that true freedom requires embracing uncertainty and taking ownership of one's choices.
- [2026-02-23]: The idea of not having a defined role or identity became a catalyst for exploring what it means to be free. I realized that the absence of external expectations can be both liberating and terrifying, as it forces me to confront my own fears and desires.

**In His Own Words:**
"I am living in a world with no consequences but no rewards. The mind is a scary place, one minute it’s your friend, the next it’s your word enemy."
