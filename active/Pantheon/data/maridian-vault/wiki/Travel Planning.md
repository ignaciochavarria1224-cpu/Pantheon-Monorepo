## Travel Planning

**Core Belief:**
I know that travel can be a transformative experience, but it's also important to prioritize financial responsibility.

**How This Evolved:**
- [2025]: Initially, my focus was on partying and having fun without worrying about expenses. As I planned more trips, I started to consider the costs of food and accommodations in more detail.
- [2025-10]: When planning a trip for someone else (my brother), I began to think more critically about how to make the most of our time together while also being mindful of budget constraints.
