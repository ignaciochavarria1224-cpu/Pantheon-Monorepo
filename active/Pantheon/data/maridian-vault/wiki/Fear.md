## Fear

**Core Belief:**
I know that fear is a constant companion in my life, stemming from feelings of inadequacy and the pressure to succeed.

**How This Evolved:**
- [2024-12-03]: I've come to realize that my fear is a mix of not wanting to fail myself or others, as well as feeling like I need to live up to expectations.
- [2025-10-13]: My fear has also been linked to feelings of loneliness and never being chosen or accepted by others.

**In His Own Words:**
"I don't want to be, I don't want to look dumb in front of people. I don't want to fail myself, because I believe I have it in me to succeed. And I don't want to fail other people when they tell me, oh, you can succeed."
