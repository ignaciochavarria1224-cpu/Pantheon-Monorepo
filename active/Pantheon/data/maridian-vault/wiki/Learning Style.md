## Learning Style

**Core Belief:**
I tend to absorb information best through hands-on experience, requiring physical engagement with the material or real-world applications to retain it.

**How This Evolved:**
- [2025]: I realized that I rely heavily on structure and planning, even if I don't always like to admit it.
- [2026]: I began to understand how my brain learns through adaptive practice tests and mirroring professor-provided materials.

**In His Own Words:**
