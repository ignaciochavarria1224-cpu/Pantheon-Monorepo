## Faith

**Core Belief:**
I believe that my success is directly tied to fulfilling God's plan for me, and if I don't achieve my full potential, I'll be letting Him and my family down.

**How This Evolved:**
- [2025]: Initially, faith was about personal responsibility and not wanting to disappoint others; later, it started exploring the idea of a higher power's design and influence on human consciousness.
- [2025]: The concept of free will and the possibility of a simulated reality began to intersect with his faith, raising questions about the nature of God's plan.

**In His Own Words:**
> "So you said something right here, maybe what you're calling the brain is the physical wiring God designed and what you call consciousness is the soul he breathed into you."
