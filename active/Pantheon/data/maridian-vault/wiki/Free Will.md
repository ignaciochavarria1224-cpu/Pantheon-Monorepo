## Free Will

**Core Belief:**
I believe that free will is a fundamental aspect of human existence, allowing individuals to make choices that shape their lives and destinies.

**How This Evolved:**
- [2025-11-22]: I've come to realize that the opinions and expectations of others can significantly impact our decisions and sense of purpose.
- [2025-12-31]: I think there's a larger, more complex force at play in shaping human behavior and events.

**In His Own Words:**
