## Identity

**Core Belief:**
I believe my identity is deeply rooted in my desire for validation from others, particularly my family and those who have uplifted me.

**How This Evolved:**
- [2026]: I began to realize that my main drive in life is to be proud of myself and make my family proud.
- [2026-02-11]: I started to feel scared about living up to the expectations of others, including myself, and the fear of being invisible.
- [2026-03-03]: My pressure comes from knowing the opportunity God has given me and believing that He has great things for me, as well as not wanting to disappoint those who have uplifted me.

**In His Own Words:**
> "I want my family to be proud of me. I think I want to be seen by them."
