## Self-Love

**Core Belief:**
I've learned that self-love is about finding what sparks passion and purpose, even when it feels elusive.

**How This Evolved:**
- [2025]: I began to realize that my search for fulfillment isn't about finding a single "calling," but rather embracing the process of exploration and growth.
- [2024]: Initially, I struggled with feeling like I needed to find something external to give me direction and purpose.

**In His Own Words:**
> "I want something I can do. I feel as though that's not something I want to do."
